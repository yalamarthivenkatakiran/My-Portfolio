About

Engineering across the seam between business problems and working software.

I'm an Environmental Systems Engineer at General Motors, working where software implementation meets systems engineering. My day-to-day is breaking business problems into solvable components, assessing requirements and their business implications, and arriving at recommended solutions with minimal supervision — alongside subject matter experts and business partners on cross-functional teams.

On the build side, I design SQL queries and stored procedures for data retrieval and transformation, write and work with RESTful APIs and formats including JSON and XML, support integrations between complex enterprise systems, and develop the data pipelines that connect them. I build React frontend components for data visualization and prototype solutions so users can explore new features before they're implemented.

A M.S. in Business Analytics (GPA 3.8) gives me the analytics foundation to pair technical delivery with business judgment. I work across functions to build consensus, promote innovation and process improvement, and communicate effectively at every stage of the SDLC.
